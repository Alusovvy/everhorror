## 1. Mine expansion (existing assets only)

**New section east of the main corridor**, built only from the Mine & Caves pack and the prop pack:

| Part | Where | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| Opened doorway | old east dead end, x ≈ 43 | 8 rock pieces that blocked it were removed |
| Timber corridor | x 44–52 | same construction as the existing corridor: planked walls, posts, beams, rails |
| **Descending slope** | x 52–64 | 3 corridor tiles tilted 10°, about 2.1 m deeper; a mine cart stopped on the slope |
| Doorway | x ≈ 67 | mine entrance frame into rock |
| **Cave chamber** | x 68–82, z −4…10 | cave walls, floor and ceiling, pillars, loose rocks, **glowing blue-iron veins** |

New examinable objects (E), with lore-flavoured placeholder text in Polish:
- *Wózek górniczy*: a mine cart half full of blue-glinting ore.
- *Żyła błękitnego żelaza*: a blue-iron vein that pulses "like breathing".
- *Porzucony kilof*: an abandoned pickaxe carved with the same mark as on grandpa's maps.
- *Kartka z szyfrem*: a cipher note in grandpa's style, with one readable line: "Nie schodźcie za błękitem." ("Don't go down past the blue.")

**How it's built:**
- The pack's sample level was copied to `scene/levels/mine.tscn`, so it's ours to edit. The pack itself is untouched.
- The extension is `scene/levels/mine_deep.tscn`, generated by `tools/generators/gen_mine_deep.gd`.

**Walkability fixes** (the level wasn't fully walkable before):
- The player's collision was a 1 m wide flat cylinder. It got stuck on the rails and couldn't fit through the ~1 m squeezes in the spawn cave arm.
- It's now a capsule with a 0.35 m radius, which rides over rails and small ledges.
- Verified with a navmesh walk test: spawn → cave arm → hall → corridor → slope → chamber, walked with real physics, no getting stuck.

## 2. Lighting and sound

**Lighting:**
- Removed the pack's sky environment and the sun light that lit the mine like the outdoors.
- New environment: black background, very low ambient light, ACES tonemapping, ambient occlusion (SSAO/SSIL), glow and volumetric fog.
- **The player carries a lamp** (warm light with a slight flicker).
- Lanterns (prop pack) hang along the corridor. Their lights flicker like flames and their cages don't throw shadows.
- The old level's 4 lights are back in their original places, now flickering.
- **The chamber is lit cold blue by the veins.** Its pillars have glowing, slowly pulsing lines, so a blue glow shows at the end of the corridor and draws the player deeper. This is my proposal; the GDD describes an orange-red pulse at the mine entrance.

**Sound:**
- Audio buses **Ambience** and **SFX**, both with cave reverb.
- The cave ambience track now loops and plays everywhere, not only near the spawn.
- Positional sounds at random intervals: water drips (6), creaking timber (5), distant rumble (2), crackle at every lantern (4).
- **Pickaxe taps from behind the chamber's far wall**, every 35–80 s: someone is digging deeper.
- ⚠️ These sounds are **placeholders I generated in code** (`tools/generators/synth_sfx.js`). They're usable, but should be replaced with real recordings (see section 3).

## Summary

The mine is now about 40 m longer and about 2 m deeper, built from the existing packs: a timber corridor, a 10° slope with a stopped mine cart, and a cave chamber with glowing blue-iron veins and 4 new examinable objects.

Lighting went from flat and outdoor-like to dark and atmospheric: a carried lamp, flickering lanterns, fog and a cold blue glow in the depths. Sound went from one ambient track to a reverberant soundscape: drips, creaks, rumbles, lantern crackle, and pickaxe taps from somewhere deeper. The sounds are code-generated placeholders to replace with real recordings.

Fixing the player's collision made the whole level walkable. All of it is verified by automated tests (navmesh walk from the spawn to the chamber, all 9 interactables) and a clean run with no errors.

The research lists CC0 and royalty-free sources for models, textures, characters with animations, sound and fonts. The best matches for this game are the Sonniss GDC bundles (sound), Quaternius (NPCs), ambientCG and Poly Haven (rock textures), and more Loafbrr packs.
